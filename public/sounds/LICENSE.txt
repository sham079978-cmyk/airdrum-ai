Drum sample licensing — AirDrum AI
==================================

The five WAV files in this folder (kick.wav, snare.wav, hihat.wav, tom.wav,
crash.wav) are NOT recordings of real drums and are NOT taken from any sample
pack. They are synthesised from scratch by the script in this repository:

    tools/generate_samples.py

Each voice uses a distinct synthesis recipe (pitch-swept sine bodies, filtered
noise bursts, high-passed metallic stacks, etc.), which is why the five sounds
are clearly different from one another rather than variations of one hit.

Because they are original computer-generated works produced by this project's
own code, they carry no third-party rights:

    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You may use, modify, redistribute and sell them, with no attribution required.

Regenerating them
-----------------
    python tools/generate_samples.py

Requires Python 3 with numpy. Output is deterministic (a fixed random seed is
used), so re-running produces byte-identical files.

Replacing them with your own samples
------------------------------------
Drop your own files in here using the same names, and update the `sound` paths
in src/drumMapping.js if you use different names. Keep the files small (they
are decoded into memory at startup) and mono or stereo WAV/MP3/OGG all work.
If you ship third-party samples, record their licence here.
