The kit

    How to play

    1. Press Start Camera and allow access when the browser asks.
    2. Wait for the status pills to read Camera ready and Hand detected.
    3. Hold a hand up, then chop down into a pad. Only a real downward swing fires a sound — holding still does nothing.
    4. After a hit, lift your hand back up to re-arm it.
    5. Use Calibration Mode to see the zones and tune sensitivity.
    6. Record a beat with ● Record, then ▶ Play it back.

    🔒 All tracking happens on your device. No camera footage ever leaves your browser.
